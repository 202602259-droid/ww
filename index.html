Four ways to learn smarter

A tool is useful when it supports a clear learning habit — not when it replaces one.

📚

Study actively

Summarize a lecture, then close your notes and answer practice questions from memory.

🔎

Research with care

Use discovery tools to find sources, then read the original papers and verify every important claim.

✍️

Write in your voice

Ask for feedback on structure and clarity. Make the argument and final decisions yourself.

Your learning comes first

Use AI responsibly.

AI can make learning more accessible, but it can also make mistakes. Stay curious, check the evidence, and follow your school's rules.

Simple rule: If you cannot explain a sentence, calculation, or claim yourself, pause and learn it before you submit it.
  • Open original sources and verify quotations, statistics, and citations.
  • Check your teacher's policy before using AI for an assignment.
  • Use AI feedback as support; keep your own analysis and voice.
  • Do not upload private, sensitive, or restricted course materials without permission.
  • Review generated work for bias, missing context, and errors.
